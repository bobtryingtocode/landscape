Landscape

Find the home the market isn't showing you.

Landscape rates every property on how good the deal really is, scores how likely a private owner is to sell, and drafts offers that go beyond the asking price — so first-time and move-up buyers stop overpaying, and agents find deals before they hit the MLS.

Works for any US town. Your data stays in this browser until you export it.

1. Describe what you need

Town, budget, acreage, size of home, must-haves. Same parameters Land Scout uses, plus a few for houses.

2. Bring in properties

Paste listings and town-record parcels (owner, acquisition date, assessed value). Every row gets a deal score and a seller-likelihood score.

3. Make a smarter offer

Compare against market comps, see where the ask is inflated, and pick a creative structure — seller financing, option, lease-purchase, and more.

How this works
Step 1 reads the state parcel map (every lot, address, land-use code, acreage). Step 2 opens each parcel's town assessor card for owner name, mailing address, assessed values, sale history and building details — it runs about one card per second, so pull a focused set. Step 3 reads any listings page you paste (LandWatch, Realtor.com, a brokerage search) and merges the address + asking price into your list, so an off-market parcel that goes on sale shows both records. Owner details are public record; use them for a purchase inquiry only.
